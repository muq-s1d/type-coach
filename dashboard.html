What These Mean

Average Your speed across all typing ever measured. One word counts as five characters.
Recent Your speed over the last minute. Blank until you have typed a few words in that window.
Best The fastest sustained stretch you have recorded, measured over at least three seconds.
Clean Share of words typed without pressing backspace. Higher means fewer corrections.
Trend One bar per day, scaled against your best day shown. Days with no typing stay blank.

Idle time is never counted. Pauses longer than three seconds are excluded, so stepping away does not change your speed either way.

What These Mean

Average
Your speed across all typing ever measured. One word counts as five characters.
Recent
Your speed over the last minute. Blank until you have typed a few words in that window.
Best
The fastest sustained stretch you have recorded, measured over at least three seconds.
Clean
Share of words typed without pressing backspace. Higher means fewer corrections.
Trend
One bar per day, scaled against your best day shown. Days with no typing stay blank.

Idle time is never counted. Pauses longer than three seconds are excluded, so stepping away does not change your speed either way.

Privacy

  • Nothing you type ever leaves this device.
  • Password, one time code and payment fields are never observed.
  • Words containing digits or symbols are discarded immediately.
  • Only words you type at least three times are ever stored.
  • The order you typed words in is never recorded, so your text cannot be reconstructed.

The only permission this extension requests is storage. It has no network access at all.

Data
